Rules & scoring

Card actions

  • A–6: Replace one of your cards.
  • 7–8: See one of your own cards.
  • 9–10: See someone else’s card.
  • Jack: Replace one of yours with another player’s card without seeing it.
  • Queen: Replace one of yours with another player’s card after seeing it.
  • King: Flush it.

Card points

Play rules

  • You can only pick up from the deck.
  • If someone discards a card—for example, a 6—and you have a 6 among your four cards, you may discard your 6 too. This lets you reduce your hand to three cards.
  • If you know another player has a matching card—for example, a 6—you may discard that card for them and replace it with one of your cards. This also lets you reduce your hand to three cards.
