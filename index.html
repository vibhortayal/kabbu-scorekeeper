Kabbu

How to play, scoring, and a simple scorekeeper.

How to play

Kabbu is a 4-player memory card game. Lowest total wins.

  1. Each player is dealt 4 cards, arranged in a 2x2 face-down grid.
  2. At the beginning of the game, each player looks at their bottom two cards.
  3. On your turn, draw a card. You cannot pick up from the discard pile.
  4. Use action cards to peek, swap, replace, or discard a King.
  5. When you think you have the lowest hand, call Kabbu.
  6. Everyone reveals. Lowest unique score wins the round.

Action cards

  • A-6: replace one of your cards
  • 7-8: see one of your own cards
  • 9-10: see someone else's card
  • Jack: swap one of yours with another player's without seeing
  • Queen: swap one of yours with another player's after seeing
  • King: put the King in the discard pile

Matching discards

  1. After a card is put in the discard pile, any player can discard a card from their hand if it matches the discarded card.
  2. If they make a mistake, they take a penalty.
  3. Once everyone is done discarding matching cards from their own hands, players may discard a matching card from someone else's hand if they know it, in turn order.

Scoring

  • Ace = -1
  • 2 to 10 = face value
  • Jack and Queen = 10
  • King = 30
  • Penalty (wrong card or seeing a card out of turn) = 30
  • If you call Kabbu and do not have the lowest score, even if tied, you get 50 points

Examples

Hand total: Ace + 4 + King = -1 + 4 + 30 = 33
Good call: You call Kabbu with 8. Others have 12, 15, and 20. You win the round.
Tied call: You call Kabbu with 10. Another player also has 10. You did not have the unique lowest score, so you get 50.
Penalty: You look at a card out of turn. Add 30 to that round.
